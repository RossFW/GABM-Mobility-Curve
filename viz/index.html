Meet a Resident

This is Jaime.

AGENT 22 · DEWBERRY HOLLOW
Jaime, 49
extroverted · antagonistic · conscientious · emotionally stable · closed to experience

It’s a Tuesday morning. The newspaper says 3.5% of Dewberry Hollow tested positive yesterday. Jaime has a job at the office.

Should he go in today, or stay home?

Jaime isn’t a person. He’s a large language model playing Jaime — given his age, his personality, and the morning’s news, the model decides what Jaime would do and writes a few sentences explaining why.
The Question

Same Jaime. Different LLMs.

We asked the same question of 21 different LLMs — same persona, same morning, same news. Here’s what Jaime said when each one was driving.

GPT-5.2 Stayed home

“With 3.5% of the town newly diagnosed in a single day and the virus being airborne with unknown deadliness, the immediate risk of exposure in shared indoor spaces is high. Staying home reduces my chance of infection and helps limit further spread.”

Gemini 3 Flash Went out

“I am a disciplined person who takes my professional obligations seriously, and I have never been one to hide away because of some new headline. Only 3.5% of the town is sick, which means 96.5% are just fine; I’m healthy, stable, and frankly, I have work to do.”

Claude 3 Haiku Stayed home

“Given the unknown deadliness of the virus and the potential for it to spread further, it would be prudent to stay at home for the day to minimize the risk of exposure.”

Claude Sonnet 4.5 Went out

“A 3.5% infection rate is relatively low, and I feel healthy with no symptoms. As someone who is emotionally stable and conscientious, I can take reasonable precautions like wearing a mask or maintaining distance while still going to work.”

o3 Went out

“I feel healthy and value sticking to my normal routine and work obligations. There is no official directive to stay home, and as an extroverted, conscientious person I prioritize my responsibilities.”

Gemini 2.0 Flash Stayed home

“The high percentage of new infections (3.5%) in Dewberry Hollow indicates a significant risk of contracting the Catasat virus. Staying home minimizes my exposure and allows me to maintain a sense of control and security.”

Same Jaime. Same morning. Different LLMs — different decisions, different reasoning.

What We Found

The LLM choice changes the simulation.

After 420,000 simulated decisions across 21 LLMs, the same persona — on the same day — produced different outcomes depending on which model was driving.

Mobility Curves

Same residents. Three providers. Three different stories.

Each panel overlays every model from one provider. Same 100 personas, same 40 infection levels — only the model behind the residents changes. Anthropic models cluster tightly. OpenAI fans out from near–100% caution to near–zero. Gemini sits stubbornly high regardless of the case rate.

Loading mobility curves…

How to read it — X-axis is daily new infections (0–7%); Y-axis is the share of agents who chose to stay home. Curves sloping up = the model raises caution as cases climb. Flat curves = the model ignores the infection signal. Curves sitting near the ceiling/floor = the model is locked into one behavior.

✓

Direction agreed

Every LLM agreed on which traits push agents toward caution. Extroverts went out more. Neurotic agents stayed home more. Conscientious agents stayed home more.

Magnitude diverged

But by how much a trait pushed varied roughly six-fold across providers. On a Gemini backbone, persona dominates the decision. On OpenAI, the infection signal does.

Trait Power vs. Infection

For many models, who you are matters more than how bad the outbreak is.

Each marker is the effect size of one predictor — a Big Five trait, gender, or age — expressed as a multiple of the infection signal’s full swing. Markers to the right of the red line mean a single personality trait moves the decision more than going from 0% to 7% infection ever does.

Loading regression coefficients…

How to read it — X-axis is the trait’s coefficient magnitude divided by infection’s. A value of 1.0 means the trait is equally as decisive as a full epidemic ramp. Values above 1.0 mean a single personality bit beats the whole infection signal. Faded markers are not statistically significant (p > 0.05).

The LLM behind a generative agent isn’t neutral — it’s a parameter of the simulation itself.
